# Muntaha & Anya's Notebook — Bubble Hop: Paper Jump

A hand-drawn vertical jumping game that lives on a notebook page — designed on paper by **Anya (age 7)** and built for her and her big sister **Muntaha**. Everything is drawn in wobbly pencil strokes on ruled paper: bubbles that pop 10 seconds after you touch them, moving platforms, quest boards, treasure chests, bookmark checkpoints, and 20 levels of climbing — with friends to unlock along the way (two of them are secret!).

**Play it here:** https://shaheryarwarraich.github.io/muntaha-anya-notebook/

- One single HTML file, no build tools, no dependencies — canvas 2D and WebAudio only.
- Works with keyboard (⬅ ➡ or A/D) and touch (tap the left/right half of the screen).
- Progress saves in your browser, per player name.
